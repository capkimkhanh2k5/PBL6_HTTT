## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a **Coastal Contemporary** visual language built for a premium maritime sports and marine excursion booking platform. It fuses the open, refreshing atmosphere of the ocean with the structured confidence of high-end travel commerce.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Vital & Ocean-Infused:** Evoking the clarity, freshness, and motion of coastal waters through vivid aquatic tones balanced against crisp, clean surfaces.
- **Prestige & Trust:** Communicating safety, certified maritime expertise, and premium hospitality with structured grid alignments, precise line work, and deep marine contrasts.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Using vibrant coral accents to draw immediate attention to calls-to-action, status indicators, and booking urgency without compromising elegance.

### Design Movement
The visual approach blends **Modern Coastal Minimalism** with **Light Glassmorphism** and soft surface stratification. Surfaces leverage pure whites and mist-tinted backdrops, crisp cool-grey borders, and subtle ocean-tinted drop shadows rather than heavy structural borders or warm textures. Warm creams, yellows, and earthy beiges are strictly avoided to preserve a pristine, refreshing sea-breeze atmosphere.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored by the deep, oxygenated tones of coastal waters and the vivid contrast of marine coral reefs. Warm yellow or cream undertones are explicitly banned in favor of pristine, cool-spectrum neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#087F8C` - Ocean Teal):** Anchors brand presence, primary interactive controls, search trigger states, active navigation tabs, and verified badges.
- **Secondary (`#FF735C` - Coral Accent):** Reserved for high-conversion actions, live availability tags, pricing highlights, promotion flags, and dynamic booking alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#102F3A` - Deep Navy):** The structural dark tone used for high-contrast typography, footer landscapes, dark header variants, and high-impact navigational bars.
- **Neutral Backgrounds & Base:**
  - **Canvas Pure:** `#FFFFFF` for primary card backgrounds, booking sheets, and interactive modal surfaces.
  - **Mist Light (`#F4F9FA` / `#F8FAFC`):** Alternating section backgrounds, inactive pill backgrounds, and structural wrapper containers.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Ultra-crisp, cool slate-grey lines that maintain structure across desktop grids without heavy visual weight.
- **Text & Contrast Tiers:**
  - **Headings & Body Primary:** `#102F3A` (Deep Navy) for AAA legibility against pure white.
  - **Muted & Metadata:** `#52707D` (Slate Teal) for secondary details, timestamps, and secondary card captions.
  - **Disabled / Subtle:** `#94A3B8` for input placeholders and inactive icons.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances avant-garde structural elegance with flawless interface readability.

### Type Pairing
- **Display & Headings (`Outfit`):** Modern, geometric, and crisp. Used for hero impact, package titles, collection headings, buttons, and numeric KPI cards. Features uniform stroke weights, elegant curve balance, and flawless Vietnamese diacritics without text clipping.
- **Body, UI & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, humanist, and airy. Delivers optimal scanning performance across tour itineraries, multi-step booking bars, pricing tables, and filter panels.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Hero & Curated Showcase:** Use `display-xl` and `display-lg` paired with `body-xl` lead paragraphs.
- **Card Titles & Section Dividers:** Set package names and category headers using `headline-md` or `headline-sm` in `Outfit` semi-bold.
- **Technical & Commerce Metadata:** Duration tags, departure times, capacity limits, and pricing tags must use `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`label-lg` / `label-md`) with high-contrast semi-bold weights to guarantee rapid visual parsing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is tailored for desktop displays at 1440px viewport widths with a crisp 12-column responsive layout.

### Desktop Grid Structure (1440px Canvas)
- **Container Max Width:** 1280px centered.
- **Columns:** 12 equal-width columns.
- **Gutter:** 24px (`1.5rem`) providing ample breathing room for media-rich adventure cards.
- **Horizontal Margin:** 80px (`5rem`) outer canvas safety offset.

### Vertical Rhythm & Section Spacing
- **Section Block Gaps:** 72px (`4.5rem`) to 96px (`6rem`) between major experience groupings to maintain a relaxed, airy resort aesthetic.
- **Component Interiors:** Built strictly on an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 32px).
- **Sticky Filter & Booking Bars:** 16px padding on vertical axes, floating 24px above bottom-screen edge on scroll triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth mirrors sunlight filtering through clear coastal water: layered, translucent, and softly diffused rather than hard-edged or murky.

### Shadow Specifications
- **Level 1 (Subtle / Card Rest - `shadow-sm`):**  
  `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.03)`  
  Used for baseline listing cards, input groups, and standard segmented controls.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Interactive - `shadow-md`):**  
  `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.04)`  
  Applied on card hover states, floating booking date pickers, and active filter popovers. Features an Ocean Teal tint.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Sticky Navigation - `shadow-lg`):**  
  `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.04)`  
  Applied to the sticky booking engine bar, floating cart drawer, and experience confirmation dialogs.

### Glassmorphism & Overlays
- **Translucent Nav Header:** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and border bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Image Gradient Overlay:** Ocean-depth scrim using `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(16, 47, 58, 0) 40%, rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.75) 100%)` for text legibility over activity photography.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy employs generous, friendly radii reminiscent of sea stones, hydrofoil curves, and contemporary luxury design.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Input Fields, Compact Buttons & Badges:** `8px` (`rounded-md`).
- **Standard Action Buttons & Form Cards:** `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Tour Cards, Gallery Media & Experience Blocks:** `20px` to `24px` (`rounded-2xl`).
- **Pills, Chips & Filter Tags:** `9999px` (Full pill shape) for fluid interactive toggling.
- **Image Masks:** Nested radius rule applies (outer radius minus container padding equals child media radius) to avoid awkward visual seams.

- **Conversion Highlight (Instant Reserve):** Coral (`#FF735C`) background, white text, 14px 28px padding. Hover: `#E8624C` with `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(255, 115, 92, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1.5px border `#087F8C`, text `#087F8C`. Hover: background `#F4F9FA`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#102F3A`. Hover: background `#F4F9FA`, text `#087F8C`.

### Form Fields & Booking Inputs
- **Container Style:** Height 52px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `12px`.
- **Typography:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px (`body-md`), text `#102F3A`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#087F8C`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.15)`.
- **Lead Search Bar (Desktop 1440px):** Unified segmented search container containing Location, Water Activity Type, Date, and Guests with vertical `#E2E8F0` divider lines and an integrated Coral search trigger button.

- **Media Header:** 16:10 aspect ratio image with top-right pill badge (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, background `rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.6)`, text white, duration/difficulty indicator).
- **Card Body:** Category label in Ocean Teal uppercase `label-sm`, title in `Syne` 18px bold `#102F3A`, pricing anchor in bold 20px with secondary Coral emphasis (`#FF735C`).
- **Interactive State:** `translateY(-4px)` with transition `0.25s ease`, shadow shifts to `shadow-md` with subtle teal glow.
