## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an avant-garde tropical adventure interface engineered for booking high-touch ocean activities along the central coast of Vietnam. It fuses coastal serenity with vibrant kinetic optimism, balancing the rhythmic motion of the sea against modern structural clarity.

The visual direction marries modern organic fluidity with high-contrast editorial typography. Rather than leaning into generic travel app aesthetics, the interface communicates warmth, ocean velocity, and premium hospitality through buoyant forms, soft coral bursts, and deep maritime tones. Surfaces evoke seafoam, sunlit shallows, and wet sand, creating an inviting, tactile environment where exploration feels effortless and sun-drenched.

## Colors

The color palette sets up a high-energy dialogue between deep oceanic currents and sun-scorched reef tones. 

- **Primary (`#FF735C` - Soft Coral):** The energetic heartbeat of the system. Reserved for primary conversion triggers, urgent badges, live availability flags, and striking micro-interactions.
- **Secondary (`#087F8C` - Oceanic Teal):** Represents deep coastal waters. Used for brand markers, active states, search parameters, selected pills, and structural accents.
- **Tertiary (`#102F3A` - Deep Navy):** The structural anchor. Used for maximum-contrast editorial typography, prominent headings, and grounding footers.
- **Neutral Surface Tier:**
  - `Base Canvas`: `#FAF9F6` (Ivory Sand) provides a natural, non-sterile backdrop with zero glare under bright mobile sunlight.
  - `Raised Surface`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White) defines interactive cards, floating toolbars, and dialog layers.
  - `Coastal Mist Tones`: `#F0F5F6` and `#E2EFF1` serve as muted fills for nested status chips, condition monitors, and alternating editorial sections.
- **Dividers & Outlines:** `#D1E5E7` and `#E2E8F0` provide crisp, low-friction boundaries that prevent visual clutter while preserving modular structure.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the kinetic, wide-stance expression of **Syne** with the balanced geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines (Syne):** Provides an adventurous editorial look. The expanded letterforms inject coastal grandeur into package titles, section headers, and promotional callouts. Headings above 24px use tight, controlled negative tracking to maintain punch and cohesion.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances Syne’s theatrical qualities with functional readability. Its rounded apertures harmonize with pill-shaped surfaces, rendering equipment lists, tide schedules, coordinates, and fine pricing clearly across high-glare environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by an 8pt spatial grid built around generous breathing room, mimicking coastal horizons and open water. 

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` outer safe margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and margins, ensuring cards and pill inputs stretch edge-to-edge with controlled tap padding.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Break standard grid uniformity by using editorial split sections: heavy headline blocks on one flank paired with dynamic 4:3 image cascades on the other. 
- **Sticky & Floating Zones:** Key controls—such as the floating search capsule and ocean condition banner—remain anchored at the top and bottom with explicit safe-area padding (`env(safe-area-inset)`).

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation departs from traditional muddy grey drops, opting instead for aqua-tinted ambient glows and layered physical buoyancy.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Surface flush with `#FAF9F6`. Outlined with 1px solid `#E2E8F0` or `#D1E5E7`.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Used for activity cards and filter chips. 
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.04);`
- **Level 2 (Floating & Hover):** Applied to hovering cards, sticky floating search widgets, and active filters.
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(8, 127, 140, 0.08);`
- **Level 3 (Modal & Sheet):** Applied to AI assistant side-drawers, ocean weather alert models, and booking drawers.
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(16, 47, 58, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(255, 115, 92, 0.08);`
- **Glassmorphism:** Navigation bars and floating query capsules use background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`) over an 85% translucent white base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The design uses ultra-pill, buoyant geometry across every layer. Components borrow the erosion-smoothed edges of sea glass and surfboards.

- **Base Components (Level 3 - Pill):** Buttons, badges, price indicators, status chips, and input text containers use full pill radiuses (`9999px` or minimum `1rem`).
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg`):** Experience cards, weather panels, and AI chat shells employ a distinct `2rem` (32px) corner radius, reinforcing a friendly, organic profile.
- **Hero Banners & Floating Search Bars (`rounded-xl`):** Large layout modules feature an expansive `3rem` (48px) radius to frame photography naturally without harsh geometric pinch points.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Coral Button:** Full pill contour, background `#FF735C`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold `label-lg`. On hover: subtle scale transform `scale(1.02)` and a soft coral glow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(255, 115, 92, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Oceanic Button:** Pill contour, background `#F0F5F6`, text `#087F8C`, border `1.5px solid #D1E5E7`. Hover: `#E2EFF1` with border `#087F8C`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Full pill, transparent background, text `#102F3A`, hover with `#F0F5F6`.

### Search Widget Floating Bar
A buoyant capsule centered over the hero or pinned to the header on scroll. Split into segmented interactive nodes:
- *Location/Beach*, *Experience Type*, *Date & Tide*, and a circular `#FF735C` search trigger.
- Enclosed within a frosted `#FFFFFF` shell with `3rem` rounded corners, Level 2 elevation, and thin `#D1E5E7` border.

### Experience Cards
- **Image Frame:** Locked at a 4:3 aspect ratio with an inner `1.5rem` corner radius, displaying lazy-loaded ocean imagery.
- **Floating Overlays:** Top-left contains an oceanic badge (e.g., `#087F8C` for "Verified Local Guide"); top-right features a circular ivory wishlist button.
- **Content Block:** Editorial title in Syne (`headline-sm`), duration and fitness ratings in Plus Jakarta Sans (`body-sm`), anchored at the bottom by a high-contrast coral price chip (`#FF735C` text over `#FFF1EE` pill background).

### Chips & Badges
- Fully rounded pills (`padding: 0.35rem 0.85rem`).
- Condition Badges: Green/Teal mist for optimal tides (`#F0F5F6`, `#087F8C`), coral mist for limited slots or rough seas (`#FFF1EE`, `#FF735C`).

### Weather & Ocean Condition Status Widget
- Compact pill-card with `#F0F5F6` fill and `#D1E5E7` perimeter border.
- Real-time surf swell, tide graph, and UV indexes shown with micro icons, teal progress bars, and high-legibility bold metrics.

### AI Assistant Chat Preview Card
- Floating drawer with `2rem` corner radius, resting on Ivory Sand with a frosted edge.
- Coral-accented chat initiator badge with conversational prompts (e.g., "Find best surfing time tomorrow at My Khe") set in deep navy text bubbles against ivory backdrops.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Pill-shaped fields with `#FAF9F6` background, inset shadows, and bold placeholder labels that slide upward on focus. Focus rings use `#087F8C` with 3px soft aqua spread.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smooth rounded tokens with `#087F8C` fills and crisp white check icons.

### Navigation & Footer
- **Sticky Navigation:** Translucent ivory-white pill docked at the screen top with micro-blur, housing an energetic wordmark and rapid booking actions.
- **Minimalist Footer:** Grounded in `#102F3A` Deep Navy with crisp white text, soft teal sub-links, and clean sustainability & safety certifications.
